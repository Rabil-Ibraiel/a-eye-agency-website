# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Prospective clients—especially founders, brand leaders, and marketing teams—evaluating a creative partner for campaigns, visual systems, motion, and art-directed content production.

## Product Purpose

A-Eye. is the public website for a three-person full-service creative content agency. It must make the studio’s creative range and point of view tangible, let visitors explore representative work, explain what the team can deliver, and turn serious interest into an honest project inquiry.

## Positioning

A small senior creative team carries an idea from human-led direction through AI-assisted production, design, motion, and final delivery. AI is a production capability inside a controlled creative process, not the product being sold.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive through referrals, social links, shared case studies, and search. They scan the opening experience, assess the quality of the work, inspect services or individual cases, and either contact the studio or share a project URL with collaborators.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Core capabilities: creative direction and content strategy, AI content production, motion design and post-production, brand and visual design, and campaign/social content systems.
- Public routes include Home, Work, case studies, Services, service details, About, Contact, and Privacy.
- The site is a Next.js App Router application and must remain fast, responsive, keyboard accessible, and usable with reduced motion.
- Exploration may be unconventional, but conventional navigation and direct URLs must remain available.
- The site must not hijack scrolling, autoplay sound, fabricate clients or results, or present concept work as paid client work.
- Inquiry success may only appear after a real successful submission.

## Brand Commitments

- Public name: **A-Eye.**
- The existing visual identity is not binding; the user explicitly authorized a complete replacement visual world.
- Voice remains direct, intelligent, concise, human, and client-focused.
- The experience should feel highly art-directed and culturally current, never like an AI-tool reseller or generic freelance template.

## Evidence on Hand

- Three production-quality fictional studies, clearly labelled as fictional projects:
  - Open Distance / Sahra Mobility
  - Room for Sound / Morrow Audio
  - Stay With the Light / Aster House
- Each study has multiple local WebP images and structured case-study copy.
- There are no verified client names, testimonials, awards, performance results, founder biographies, or showreel assets; the interface must not invent them.

## Product Principles

- Demonstrate taste through the work instead of claiming superiority.
- Keep human direction legible wherever AI production is discussed.
- Make exploration memorable without hiding information or actions.
- Treat motion as one coherent narrative material, not decoration attached to sections.
- Preserve truthful labels, responsive access, and fast route navigation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The public site must support keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic structure, meaningful image alternatives, 44px minimum touch targets, sufficient contrast, and a complete reduced-motion path with the same content and actions.
